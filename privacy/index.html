Privacy

Privacy on this site

Updated 26 October 2026

Short version: this site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, ads or forms, and it does not ask you for any personal information. The only data that leaves your browser is what any website or embedded video needs to work, described below.

What this site itself collects

Nothing. There is no visitor counter, no analytics script, no newsletter sign-up and no contact form (only a plain email address). Fonts, images and styles are served from this site's own address.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in server logs for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

YouTube videos

Recordings are played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Video previews and thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's servers, which receive your IP address. YouTube says it does not store cookies for viewing in this mode until you start a video. Once you press play, Google's privacy policy applies to that video.

Ko-fi and other links

The “Buy me a coffee” links lead to Ko-fi, a separate service. If you choose to support the recordings there, Ko-fi handles the payment and its own privacy policy applies. We only see what Ko-fi shares with creators, such as the name and message you choose to leave.

Email

If you write to hello [at] nomadechosounds.com, your message and email address are seen only by Andrey, used only to reply, and never shared or added to any list.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or anything else that collects data, this page will say so first, with the date of the change.
