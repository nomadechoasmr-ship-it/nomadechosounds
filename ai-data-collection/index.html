AI Training Data Collection in Kazakhstan & Central Asia

A developing service of Nomad Echo Sounds

Nomad Echo Sounds is starting a new business direction: collecting real-world audio, video, photo and language material in Kazakhstan and Central Asia for teams that build and train AI models. It grows out of our field recording work in the Tian Shan mountains and on the Kapchagay reservoir, where we already record, check and document every take ourselves.

This is an early-stage service. We are not offering ready-made datasets. Each project is scoped with the client and collected to order.

Possible categories

These are directions we can discuss, not a fixed product list.

Where we stand today

  • Field experience. Our nature recordings are made in person and checked before use. How we work is shown in our field notes and on the measurement data page.
  • Our own equipment. Audio on a Zoom H5 (WAV 48 kHz / 24-bit), video on an iPhone 16 Pro in 4K.
  • Contributor network: being formed. We are building a network of people in the region who can record and film to a brief. It is at the formation stage.

How a project is agreed

Capabilities, volumes and technical requirements are discussed individually for each project: formats, quality, metadata, locations, deadlines, as well as consent, licensing and data handling terms. Write to us with what you need, and we will tell you honestly what we can deliver and on what timeline.

Contact

Email: hello@nomadechosounds.com. Please describe the type of data, the approximate volume, the format and any deadlines.
